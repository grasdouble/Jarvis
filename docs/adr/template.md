## Context and decision needed

Describe the observed problem, constraints, source/base revision, relevant accepted baseline and uncertainties. Separate facts from estimates and proposed direction.

## Options and trade-offs

Describe viable options and consequences for scope, compatibility, isolation, permissions, memory/data, cost, delivery and operations as applicable. Omit genuinely inapplicable topics; do not add boilerplate `N/A` lists.

## Decision and rationale

State the proposed or actually accepted choice and why. Identify unresolved assumptions and preserved constraints; do not declare authority from this document itself.

## Consequences and verification

Record positive/negative consequences, risks, alternatives rejected, actual verification/evidence versus future acceptance, migration/recovery needs and affected documents/backlog as applicable. Keep design acceptance distinct from implementation and activation. State only checks actually performed; do not invent acceptance or verification to fill a field.

## Acceptance and history

When acceptance, rejection, or supersession applies, record the actual owner/actor, date, approved scope, reviewed revision and stable reference or recoverable evidence. Until acceptance evidence is available, state Pending and the exact required input. Preserve native/current-head approval gates. Record applicable rejection/supersession rationale and linked replacement history without inventing dates or rewriting the old decision. Omit inapplicable lifecycle details rather than asserting `None` or adding boilerplate.

## Follow-up

Name bounded dependent work, remaining decision/verification owner and resumption conditions. A decision record alone does not start implementation, publish GitHub effects or activate a runtime change.
